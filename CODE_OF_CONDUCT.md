# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Flowtract contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to a welcoming,
professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas and evidence.
- Protect private, security-sensitive, and personal information.
- Accept maintainer moderation and correction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation.
- Retaliation against reporters or reviewers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or temporarily/permanently restrict
participation that violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporters from retaliation.

For a private conduct report, contact the repository owner through their GitHub
profile and request a private channel without posting report details. After the
Flowtract organization is established, this document will name the dedicated
private moderation channel.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing Flowtract publicly.
